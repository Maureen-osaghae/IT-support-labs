# IT Support Home Labs

Hello, I'm Maureen Osaghae, based in Birmingham, UK. I'm moving into IT support from a Medical Laboratory Science and small business background. I hold CompTIA A+ and Security+, and I'm building hands-on labs to practise the work I want to do: fixing problems, managing accounts and access, and writing down what I learn.

Each lab here is a real build, with my own screenshots, the problems I hit, and how I solved them. I only list a lab as complete once I've finished it.

## How each lab is written

- **Goal:** what I'm trying to set up or fix
- **Build steps:** what I did, with screenshots
- **Problems and fixes:** what went wrong and how I found the cause
- **Tests:** what I checked, and whether it passed
- **What I learned:** the short version I'd tell a colleague
